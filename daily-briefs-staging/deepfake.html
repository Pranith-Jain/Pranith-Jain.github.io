Forensic Insight of the Day

Across multiple incidents, the decisive inflection point is not high-fidelity voice cloning but enrollment or support workflow abuse over trusted channels (Teams calls, helpdesks, and passkey registration). Prioritizing telemetry and approvals on “who can change who’s factors” often outperforms deepfake detection in stopping real-world compromises.
